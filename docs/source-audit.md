# Source audit and field coverage

Before enabling a provider, record competition, season, sample date range, expected and observed match counts, fields present, corrections behavior, access terms, retrieval time, and audit reviewer in `manifests/source_registry.csv`. Sample at least 30 matches across seasons and include playoffs, five-set matches, postponements, and missing-stat cases. Do not assume a provider's league listing guarantees historical box-score or odds coverage.

Suggested authoritative tiers:

1. FIVB VIS documented service for international event schedules/results and public match documents.
2. Official league websites and authorized league feeds for club records.
3. API-Volleyball as a convenience source only after endpoint-by-endpoint audit.
4. Authorized DataVolley files for rally/touch/rotation detail. OpenVolley parser documentation describes the file semantics; it is not a source of match files.
5. Odds provider selected only after verifying sport, market, bookmaker, timestamp frequency, historical depth, and permitted retention. Do not assume The Odds API has volleyball coverage.

Source conflicts are retained and reconciled field-by-field; provenance is never discarded.

## Selected club-league archive audit (November 2026)

- Brazil CBV Data Project lists Superliga men/women seasons from 2017/18 through 2024/25. The corrected parser uses each match row's own parent phase and round IDs; the previous parser incorrectly read the first round ID repeated elsewhere in each row. Across 16 official archived pages, 2,113 regular-phase fixture IDs are enumerated and 2,101 have final results (99.43%). The 2022/23 women's schedule lists 133 IDs; retain the source count as an anomaly. The 2019/20 men's page includes six scheduled but unscored games during the pandemic interruption. Source hashes and per-season rates are recorded in `data/coverage/cbv_seasons.csv`. Sources: [CBV history index](https://cbv-web.dataproject.com/MainHome.aspx), [CBV 2024/25 regulations](https://wp.cbv.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/199.2-REC-REGULAMENTO-ESPECIFICO-DA-COMPETICAO-SUPERLIGA-TEMPORADA-2024.2025.pdf).
- Italy men's 2024/25 official round calendar is available at [Lega Volley](https://ww2.legavolley.it/Calendario.asp?Anno=2024&IdCampionato=947). The downloaded page includes match scores and postponed fixtures, so completed and not-yet-played rows need separate status handling.
- Italy women's 2024/25 official schedule PDF is available at [Lega Volley Femminile](https://www.legavolleyfemminile.it/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Calendario_Serie-A1_-24-25_Esteso-3.pdf). Its official club result pages expose season and round filters; result extraction remains pending.
- PlusLiga reports that it opened digital historical statistics from 2000/01; the league notes early seasons 2000/01–2007/08 have less detailed aggregate data. The official 2024/25 regular-season PDF enumerates match IDs 001–240 with dates, teams and match results. The normalized slice contains all 240 IDs (100% of the official enumerated regular-season total); dates are available for 176/240 rows (73.33%). Set-point columns were excluded because the PDF text layer contains malformed/contradictory set values. Playoffs are outside this denominator. Sources: [official 2024/25 match PDF](https://www.plusliga.pl/dl/625351/fd7c66/Faza_Zasadnicza_PL_2024-2025.pdf), [league archive announcement](https://www.tauronliga.pl/news%2Cplusliga-uruchomila-cyfrowe-statystyki-od-pierwszego-zawodowego-sezonu.html).

The Italian men’s 2024/25 official calendar provides both 11-round legs. The regular-season denominator is 132 matches (12 teams, double round robin); playoffs are excluded. Both official pages are archived as `data/bronze/italy_try0.html` (first leg) and `data/bronze/italy_m_24_25.html` (return leg), and normalized records are deduplicated by official `IdGara` in `data/silver/italy_superlega_m_2024_25.csv`. The audited regular-season slice is 132/132 (100%). This is one season’s regular-season result coverage, not a claim of complete historical or playoff data.

## Backfill from 2000

### CEV 2000/01 parsing correction

The full official 2000/01 LegList pages are now imported for both divisions. The men's list has 48 `L-001..048` Main Phase IDs and 8 `4-001..008` matches in CEV's “4th Final”; the women's list has 48 `L-301..348` and 8 `4-301..308` IDs. The official Final Four phase adds four `F-001..004` men's and `F-301..304` women's results, giving 60 match IDs per gender. Earlier phase pulls overlap 16 IDs with each full list; records are merged by competition, gender and official match ID, with full-list provenance preferred for overlapping IDs. The inventory denominator is therefore 60 for each gender; observed results are 60/60 in the generated snapshot. Rebuild via `volley-data import-cev-leglist` using the archived HTML inputs; the importer stores source URL and SHA-256. Official sources: [men full LegList](https://www-old.cev.eu/Competition-Area/CompetitionView.aspx?CID=0&ID=70&PID=0&type=LegList), [men 4th Final phase](https://www-old.cev.eu/Competition-Area/Competition.aspx?ID=70&PID=159), [men Final Four](https://www-old.cev.eu/Competition-Area/Competition.aspx?ID=70&PID=160), [women full LegList](https://www-old.cev.eu/Competition-Area/CompetitionView.aspx?CID=0&ID=73&PID=0&type=LegList), [women 4th Final phase](https://www-old.cev.eu/Competition-Area/Competition.aspx?ID=73&PID=167), [women Final Four](https://www-old.cev.eu/Competition-Area/Competition.aspx?ID=73&PID=168). The former zero-women count came from `W`/`F` and `2000/2001`/`2000/01` label mismatches in the aggregation and has also been corrected.

### CEV reuse and publication limits

The CEV terms restrict copying, downloading, and sharing to uses explicitly permitted for personal, non-commercial use. The multi-season CEV result expansion and raw HTML therefore remain local and gitignored unless a separate license or written permission allows redistribution. The repository may publish importer code, source URLs, hashes, aggregate coverage metadata, and tests; do not publish bulk CEV match rows or raw pages by default. Treat `data/coverage_cev.csv` as local metadata if it contains match-level counts that the applicable terms do not authorize for public redistribution.

To rebuild a local copy, keep output under ignored `data/local/`: run `volley-data import-cev --from-year 2000 --to-year 2027 --data-dir data/local --archive-dir data/bronze/cev_archive`, then the same command with `--leglists-only`, then with `--from-archive`. This collects official phase pages and all-phase LegLists, parses the cached HTML without another network request, and merges by gender, competition ID, and official match ID. The per-page URL and SHA-256 are retained in local CEV coverage metadata. The coverage audit accepts a denominator only when at least one official full LegList and every discovered phase page parsed successfully; it uses the unique official match-ID union, subtracting cancelled fixtures and non-fixture bye placeholders. Ongoing 2026/27 editions are intentionally excluded from the completed-season denominator gate.

The official CEV archive states the 2020 Champions League editions ended prematurely due to COVID-19; the CEV also announced cancellation of the remainder of the 2020 European Cups. The importer marks unplayed 2020 edition fixtures as `cancelled`, so they remain visible but are excluded from completed-result coverage denominators. See the [CEV 2020 men's archive notice](https://www-old.cev.eu/Competition-Area/Competition.aspx?ID=1193&PID=1939), [women's archive notice](https://www-old.cev.eu/Competition-Area/Competition.aspx?ID=1196&PID=1948), and [CEV announcement](https://www-old.cev.eu/News.aspx?NewsID=30632).

API-Volleyball supports league/season game queries, but historical depth varies by competition and subscription. The backfill CLI archives each raw season response and writes a per-league/year report through the current UTC year. Obtain IDs from the provider's league endpoint, audit early, middle, and recent seasons, and confirm account entitlement before a full run. A zero-row response is recorded as zero; it does not prove that the season had no matches. The report is raw response coverage, not a complete canonical dataset.

FIVB VIS documents public volleyball match-list requests with date filters and versioning. Public access does not guarantee full coverage back to 2000 or all leagues. Compare each year with official calendars, and account for fields that are not public.

The FIVB VIS scope audit maps only event titles matching men's World League/VNL or women's World Grand Prix/VNL to the corresponding annual ledger edition. It requires a finished provider status (`Closed`, `Result is official`, `Finished`, or `Corrected`) and a valid 3–0 through 3–2 match score, deduplicates by provider match ID, rejects explicit event-title/year mismatches, and excludes qualification/qualifying events and test-only records. The downloader requires each response's `NbItems` to match parsed official IDs and rejects duplicates, preventing incomplete yearly downloads from being recorded as successful. A November 6, 2026 refresh queried all 27 years from 2000–2026: all response counts matched, yielding 29,211 unique official FIVB match IDs across all event types. The selected World League/Grand Prix/VNL lineage contains 4,524 valid outcomes across 40 annual editions. One denominator is verified: Volleyball World's [2021 season review](https://en.volleyballworld.com/news/looking-back-a-historic-volleyball-nations-league-in-2021) reports 248 total matches (124 per gender), matching the 124 official VIS results for each gender; both VNL 2021 rows are 124/124. Other edition percentages remain blank pending official fixture census evidence. The [VIS tournament-list request](https://www.fivb.org/VisSDK/VisWebService/GetVolleyTournamentList.html) supports season filtering, and the official [match-list documentation](https://www.fivb.org/vissdk/fivb.vis.model/Fivb.Vis.Model~Fivb.Vis.Volley.GetVolleyMatchListRequest.html) documents response counts and pagination. The year-by-year 2000–2005 probe is recorded in [`reports/fivb_vis_archive_audit_2000_2005.md`](../reports/fivb_vis_archive_audit_2000_2005.md): matching tournament entries are absent from the VIS index in 2000–2004; 2005 World League and Grand Prix entries are listed but `IsVisManaged=0`, so no match/result records can be retrieved. Two 2021 VNL Finals entries were incorrectly returned with Season/date 2000. This is a source limitation, not evidence those events did not occur; inventory statuses therefore say `not_listed` or `listed_unmanaged`. The men's and women's VNL 2020 entries are explicitly marked cancelled per Volleyball World's [official VNL history](https://en.volleyballworld.com/news/vnl-2021-event-intro). Denominators remain blank for cancelled rows.

The published open volleyball match-score dataset described in the 2024 Data in Brief article contains 706 matches from selected men's FIVB World Cup editions only (1965–2019, intermittent); it cannot satisfy a 2000-to-current all-league requirement. The CC BY 4.0 file is included as a clearly scoped starter subset under data/. Its source CSV headers are inconsistent, so only the explicit winner field is treated as a usable outcome; other numeric fields remain unverified raw values.
