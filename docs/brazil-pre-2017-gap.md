# Brazil Superliga results before 2017/18

## 2016/17 evidence

CBV's official schedules enumerate regular-season match numbers 1–132 for each division. The women's schedule is [CBV Nota Oficial 179/16](https://wp.cbv.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/n.o.-179-3-179-cbv-tabela-oficial-superliga-feminina-2016-2017.pdf); the men's schedule is [CBV Nota Oficial 179/16](https://wp.cbv.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/n.o.-179-4-179-cbv-tabela-oficial-superliga-masculina-2016-2017.pdf). These establish the fixture denominator only.

Final scores are published separately in CBV's numbered *Notas Oficiais*, organized by round and division. Examples include men's match IDs 91–96 in [Nota 026/17](https://wp.cbv.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/n.o.-026-1-026-cbv-09.02.2017-registro-competicoes-de-quadra-cobrav-e-ucv.pdf) and 121–126 in [Nota 044/17](https://wp.cbv.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/n.o.-044-1-044-cbv-10.03.2017-registro-competicoes-de-quadra-selecoes-de-quadra-e-cobrav.pdf). A CBV [round recap](https://wp.cbv.com.br/arquivos/166649) also lists all six men's results for one return round. This confirms match-level results exist in official publications, but not a single season file.

The repository does not yet contain all 2016/17 result notices, their PDF hashes, or a reconciled 132-match result ledger. The download attempt from the workspace received HTTP 403; web-indexed official notices are readable, but do not provide local source bytes for reproducible hashing. Thus the coverage audit records **132 expected matches per division** while leaving result numerators and coverage percentages blank. Do not treat the schedule PDF itself as completed-results coverage.

## Remaining source gap

To close this slice, archive every official approved-result notice for the men's and women's regular seasons, hash each original PDF, extract its match number/date/team codes/set score, and reconcile against schedule IDs 1–132. Retain a separate phase for playoffs. No clear open-data license was found; keep raw documents and normalized bulk rows local unless CBV terms or written permission permit redistribution.

The CBV [other editions index](https://wp.cbv.com.br/superliga/outras-edicoes) lists seasons back to 2005/06, but match-level results for seasons before 2016/17 remain unverified in this repository.
