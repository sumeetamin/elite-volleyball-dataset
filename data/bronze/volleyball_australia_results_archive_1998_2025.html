Australian Volleyball League & Super League Results 1998–2025

superleague.volleyball.org.au · Results as recorded at the time. Some seasons are incomplete.

Match results from the Australian Volleyball League and its predecessor competitions, from the first season in 1998 to 2022, and the Australian Volleyball Super League from 2023 to 2025. Results are shown as they were recorded at the time. Some seasons are incomplete, and team names are given as they were at the time. In all, 2,327 games are recorded across 26 seasons; the number for each season is shown next to its heading.

Most seasons were played as best of five sets, rally point scoring to 25 (fifth set to 15). The first season in 1998 trialled a different format, and Super League seasons from 2023 use sets to 18 points; both are explained under those years.
