# Brazil Superliga women 1999/2000: archived first phase

The [contemporaneous UOL first-phase page](https://web.archive.org/web/20070610055851/http://esporte.uol.com.br/volei/superligafem9900fase1.htm) lists 22 rounds, five scored matches per round, and the final standings for 11 clubs. Its rules say the clubs played turn and return. The retained browser capture is `sources/brazil_superliga_w_1999_2000_uol_archive.tsv`; `scripts/import_brazil_w_1999_2000_uol.py` validates and imports 110 dated results. The importer resolves five abbreviated team names, verifies each club pair appears twice, and reconciles all 11 clubs' win totals against the final standings. This is a secondary contemporary archive; official CBV match sheets remain to be checked.

One source anomaly remains: the page lists Fox/Cascavel as home against Petrobras/Macaé in both legs. Both outcomes are retained, with published ordering, and the home venue should be checked against CBV sheets before using home advantage features. UOL does not expose stable match IDs, so silver IDs are explicitly derived from season, phase, round and clubs. Its source SHA-256 covers the TSV extraction, not the upstream HTML bytes.
