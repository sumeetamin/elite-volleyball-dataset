# Brazil Superliga 2005/06 UOL archive

The [women's first-phase page](https://web.archive.org/web/20070610061327/http://esporte.uol.com.br/volei/superliga/fem_jogos0506.jhtm) lists 72 dated results for nine clubs. The [men's first-phase page](https://web.archive.org/web/20070610061238/http://esporte.uol.com.br/volei/superliga/masc_jogos0506.jhtm) lists 132 for 12 clubs. The retained browser captures are `sources/brazil_superliga_w_2005_06_uol_archive.tsv` and `sources/brazil_superliga_m_2005_06_uol_archive.tsv`. `scripts/import_brazil_2005_06_uol.py` checks every club pairing twice and reconciles win totals to both published final tables.

The men's 08/02 On Line–Banespa line says 1–3, while its four set-point scores indicate 3–1 and the final standings also require an On Line win. The normalized outcome uses 3–1 and the raw line remains in the capture. Six other set-point strings conflict with the published match outcomes or contain apparent point typos; normalized data retains only the match-level set scores and dates. The source is contemporary secondary reporting; official CBV match sheets and playoffs still need independent reconciliation. IDs are deterministic derived hashes.
