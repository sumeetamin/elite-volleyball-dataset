# Brazil Superliga women 2006/07: archived match results

The [contemporaneous UOL season page](https://web.archive.org/web/20080727032525/http%3A//esporte.uol.com.br/volei/superliga/fem_jogos0607.jhtm) gives 14 regular-season rounds, four scored fixtures in each, and a final eight-team table. A browser capture of its visible score rows is retained in `sources/brazil_superliga_w_2006_07_uol_archive.tsv`; `scripts/import_brazil_w_2006_07_uol.py` validates and imports it. Every unordered pair meets twice, once at each home venue; each team plays 14 matches. The imported score winners reproduce the archived final-table wins for all eight clubs. This establishes 56/56 regular-season outcomes under the documented double round-robin format.

This is a secondary contemporary archive, rather than a CBV match-sheet export. The [CBV historical edition index](https://wp.cbv.com.br/superliga/outras-edicoes) lists 2006/07 but its image link is `#`, so it does not supply match records. Source IDs are explicitly derived from season, phase, round and teams; UOL did not expose stable match IDs in the result rows. Dates are in the source page (December 2006–February 2007).

Two rows have set-point strings that contradict the displayed match score: Vôlei Futuro–Rexona and Minas–Osasco in return round 1. The scoreboard results reconcile with the final standings; set points are excluded from silver. The source SHA-256 in silver covers the retained TSV browser extraction, not the upstream HTML bytes. Official CBV match-sheet reconciliation remains a quality task; this season's result coverage is 100% under the retained fixture census.
