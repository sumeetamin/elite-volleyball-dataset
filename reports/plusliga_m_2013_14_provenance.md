# PlusLiga men 2013/14 regular-season backfill

Official archive: [https://www.tauronliga.pl/games/tour/19.html](https://www.tauronliga.pl/games/tour/19.html). Its season page rendered 22 regular-season rounds with six match cards each, 132 total, and displayed dates, home/away teams, and completed set scores.

The captured records do not retain a visible official match ID. `source_match_id` is therefore a deterministic, namespaced SHA-256 identity derived from season/date/home/away; it is not claimed to be a PLS identifier. The bronze hash covers the retained browser extraction, not upstream HTML bytes. Cross-check: twelve teams each appear in 22 regular-season matches (132 fixtures). All dates and scores validate.
