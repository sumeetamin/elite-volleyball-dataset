# Brazil Superliga women 2004/05

The [UOL first-phase archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20070610061147/http://esporte.uol.com.br/volei/superliga/fem_jogos0405.jhtm) lists 72 dated results for nine clubs. The captured source rows are retained in `sources/brazil_superliga_w_2004_05_uol_archive.tsv`; `scripts/import_brazil_w_2004_05_uol.py` normalizes them to `data/silver/brazil_superliga_w_2004_05_uol.csv`.

Validation requires all 36 unordered club pairings to occur twice and the result-derived win totals to match the archived standings: Osasco 13, Campos 8, MRV/Minas 7, Rexona 16, Brasil Telecom 8, Macaé 1, Pinheiros 10, São Caetano 5 and Sesi 4. Thus the regular-season result coverage is 72/72 (100%). The archive is a contemporary secondary source; official CBV match protocols have not been cross-checked. Playoffs are excluded.
