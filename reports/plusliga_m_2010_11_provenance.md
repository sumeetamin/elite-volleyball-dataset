# PlusLiga men 2010/11 regular-season backfill

Official archive: [https://www.tauronliga.pl/games/tour/13.html](https://www.tauronliga.pl/games/tour/13.html). It rendered 90 completed regular-season match cards with unique official IDs, dates, home/away teams, and set scores.

The local bronze TSV retains the extracted table fields. Its SHA-256 is the hash of that retained extraction, not the upstream HTML; raw page bytes were unavailable because direct workspace DNS access is blocked. The official schedule denominator is 10 teams x 18 matches/team / 2 = 90. Every team appears 18 times; all 90 rows have valid completed scores and exact dates.
