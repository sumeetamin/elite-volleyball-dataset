# Tauron Liga women: historical source gap, 2000/01–2007/08

Checked 2026-11-07. No pre-2008/09 season was imported: this review did not locate a source that exposes a complete, season-wide regular-season match list with outcomes and a defensible denominator for any of these eight seasons.

## Search findings

- **Official current archive:** [Tauron Liga women’s match archive](https://www.tauronligakobiet.pl/games/nocookies/1.html) exposes season editions back to 2008/09 only. Its first older accessible season is 2008/09 (tour 10). Direct official season pages and the season selector were inspected; no 2007/08 or earlier edition was present.
- **Official league history:** [PLS anniversary article](https://www.tauronliga.pl/news,internetowy-jubileusz.html) says the PLPS site began as `pls.pl` in 2001 and that a separate women’s portal was created after the women’s league began. The article describes the portal history but does not provide historic match results. [PLS company history](https://www.pls.pl/historia.html) likewise gives organizational history, not match files.
- **2000/01 partial club record:** [Historia Wisły’s Seria A 2000/01 page](https://historiawisly.pl/wiki/index.php?title=Seria_A_2000%2F2001_%28siatk%C3%B3wka_kobiet%29) lists Wisła’s 18 regular-season opponents and set results, plus standings. It is one club’s schedule, not the other nine teams’ complete fixtures; it cannot independently verify 90/90 matches.
- **2007/08 partial season metadata:** [Women Volleybox 2007/08 edition](https://women.volleybox.net/women-tauron-liga-2007-08-o799) lists the dates and ten teams but reports zero match records. Its other pre-2008 pages are similarly not complete result tables.
- **Contemporary news and club histories:** searches surfaced isolated head-to-head histories and club-season summaries, including [SportoweFakty’s historic matchup report](https://sportowefakty.wp.pl/siatkowka/656096/historia-spotkan-par-14-kolejki-orlen-ligi). These confirm selected scores but do not cover every fixture. They are not safe to aggregate into a “complete” season without an independent fixture census and row-level cross-check.
- **League summary PDFs:** [PZPS/PLS season summaries](https://vis.ozps.pl/wp-content/uploads/2016/10/podsumowanie-LSK_sezon-2018-2019_c_zestawienia.pdf) contain medalists and standings/league aggregate context, not an auditable list of all fixtures. Their aggregate match totals do not distinguish all required regular-season rows from postseason phases.
- **Commercial archives:** Flashscore’s [TAURON Liga archive](https://www.flashscore.com/volleyball/poland/tauron-liga-women/archive/) exposes season winners and archive navigation only as far back as 2009/10 in the retrieved page. It did not supply 2000/01–2008/09 fixture/result rows.
- **Internet Archive:** searched the historical `pls.pl`, `lsk.net`, and `plusliga-kobiet.pl` domains and inspected archived snapshots/indexes. The `lsk.net` snapshots resolve to Lunds SK football pages, not Polish volleyball; `pls.pl` snapshots provide historic news/article pages but no confirmed complete women’s regular-season result table was found. The modern `plusliga-kobiet.pl` domain had no useful historical captures in the queried range.

**Next useful retrieval path:** request the PZPS/PLS historical match sheets or a database export for the 2000/01–2007/08 top division, with fixture IDs, dates, opponents and set scores. For 2000/01–2004/05 the competition was the top PZPS I Liga/Seria A; from 2005/06 the professional LSK existed, so those years may need separate PZPS and PLPS/archived-site records. A per-team newspaper archive is a possible fallback only if all teams’ schedules can be enumerated and reconciled against the official season format.
