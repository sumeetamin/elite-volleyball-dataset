# PlusLiga men 2009/10 reconciliation

Official standings verify 10 teams × 18 regular-season matches, for a **90-match denominator** (standings SHA-256 41b668ec7f9e34fcb1c42349fa4345121f1dbedfef47c4109b0978db6e042383). All **90/90 regular-season results and exact dates** are now reconciled by pair-archive game ID, official detail-page matchup/date, and reciprocal team-chart IDs and scores.

Some detail-page score displays conflict with the pair archive, so those displayed scores were excluded; pair-page results were accepted only when both participating teams’ charts independently matched the result and date. Postseason rows did not pass the same reconciliation and remain excluded; postseason denominators are unknown.

Per-page URLs and hashes are in [plusliga_m_2009_10_source_inventory.csv](plusliga_m_2009_10_source_inventory.csv), and normalized regular-season rows are in the local silver table. Local HTML remains in ignored data/bronze/plusliga_m_2009_10/.
